# Shards of Kala Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 10, 2026

Shards of Kala ("the game") is made by Lunar Winter ("we", "us"). This policy explains what data the
game uses and why.

## Data we collect

We do not collect, store, or sell your personal data. The game has no accounts, does not ask for
your name, email, phone number, location, contacts, photos, or microphone, and uses no analytics.

Game progress (cleared stages, coins, skins, missions, records, and settings) is stored only on
your device, in the game's internal storage. It is never sent to our servers. Uninstalling the
game or clearing its data deletes this progress.

## Device permissions

- **Vibrate (VIBRATE)**: a short vibration when menu buttons are pressed. It does not access any data.

## Ads

Settings > Google > Ads (or Settings > Privacy > Ads).

## Children

The game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from children.
